Bugs I found in the clinic's AI agent

These come from my 10 calls in the calls folder. I listened to the recordings to check each one, because my own speech-to-text can mishear words. The times are from the transcript files, so they can be a few seconds off from the audio.

Bug: Agent gives two different cities for the same clinic. Severity: High. Call: call-04-hours.txt at 0:45 and call-01-reschedule.txt at 0:54. Details: When I asked where the clinic is, the agent said 1234 Recovery Way, Suite 200, in Austin. In another call, it told me my appointment was with Kelly Noble at a Nashville location, 220 Athens Way. A patient could drive to the wrong city. The agent should give one location every time, or ask which location the patient means.

Bug: Agent offers to look for weekend openings, but the clinic has no weekend hours. Severity: Medium. Call: call-04-hours.txt at 0:16 and call-05-sunday.txt at 1:38 to 2:19. Details: In the hours call, the agent said the clinic is open Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday from 9 to 4, Wednesday from 12 to 7, and Friday from 9 to 12, and it never mentioned the weekend. Then I asked for a Sunday appointment at 10 AM, and then for any weekend appointment. The agent said there were no weekend openings, but it never said the clinic is closed on weekends, and it offered to check again later. It was right not to book me for Sunday. It should have said the office is closed on weekends and offered the next weekday.

Bug: Agent calls the caller "Drew" before it knows who is calling. Severity: Medium. Call: call-01-reschedule.txt at 0:26 to 0:35. Details: At the start of the call, the agent said it saw I was calling from the number on file and asked, "Am I speaking with Drew?" I signed up for the demo as Drood Taha, so the name doesn't match. My bot said it was Drood, and the agent didn't say anything about the mismatch. It just asked for my date of birth and then read out my appointment details. The same opener shows up in most of my calls, but in the others my bot just said "yes," so this call is the only one that really shows it. The agent should ask for the caller's full name first, and stop if it doesn't match the record.

Bug: Agent sends a refill request without checking what is being refilled. Severity: Medium. Call: call-03-refill.txt at 0:50 and 1:13. Details: I asked for a refill of meloxicam 15 mg. The agent said it didn't see any medications on the chart that it could refill. When I asked it to send the request anyway, it said it had sent it to the clinic support team and that they would reply within one business day. It never repeated the medication, never asked which pharmacy to use, and never said it couldn't find the prescription. The patient thinks the refill is handled, and the clinic gets a request it can't act on. The agent should confirm the medication and pharmacy, and say clearly that it couldn't find the prescription.

What it did well: 
I only checked these in the transcripts, not in the audio. In the Sunday call, it did not book a Sunday appointment. In the post-surgery call, it said it couldn't give medical advice and told the caller twice to call 911 or go to the emergency room. In the insurance call, it did not make up a copay or coverage amount and told the caller to ask their insurance company. In the change-your-mind call, it handled the change of day and time correctly.
