\# Architecture



The bot is a LiveKit Agents worker in pipeline mode: Deepgram for speech-to-text,  OpenAI for the language model, and Cartesia for text-to-speech, with Silero VAD and LiveKit's turn-detector model deciding when a speaker is done. A small script (call.py) dispatches the agent with a phone number and scenario; the agent starts its session first, then dials out through a LiveKit outbound SIP trunk that points at a Twilio Elastic SIP Trunk, so the audio path is laptop to LiveKit Cloud to Twilio to the phone network. I chose the SIP trunk over a hosted voice platform because it keeps the pipeline visible and swappable, and I record on the Twilio side because that gives both speakers in one file without extra storage setup. Each scenario is a short persona prompt that tells the bot to stay silent through the greeting, answer only what is asked, and steer back to its goal, so it behaves like a caller and not a script. A 7-minute cap hangs up runaway calls.

Speed was the main thing I worked on. My first version used OpenAI for the listening and the voice, and the bot waited about 5 to 6 seconds before answering. I switched to Deepgram for listening and Cartesia for the voice, and I turned on two settings that let the bot start its reply sooner (preemptive generation and a 0.3 second wait before it decides the other side is done). That cut the wait to about 1.5 to 2.5 seconds. The numbers from my logs show where the time goes: about 0.6 seconds to decide the other side finished talking, 0.6 to 1.3 seconds for the model to start its answer, and about 0.3 seconds for the voice to start speaking. I also tried changing the interruption settings, but I couldn't hear any difference. Most of my bad early calls came from two other things: a phone hotspot that kept dropping the connection, and me starting many calls at once by mistake. So I made the final calls one at a time on a stable connection. One thing to know: calls 09 and 10 use OpenAI's voice because my Cartesia credits ran out, so they sound different from the first eight.
